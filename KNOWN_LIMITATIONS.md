# Known limitations

This list is deliberately specific. It is part of the release package, not a
promise that an unmeasured behavior is finished.

## Runtime and hardware

- Snow-Burgers is WebGPU-only. It has no WebGL fallback. A browser without a
  usable WebGPU adapter receives an explanatory stop screen.
- The accepted automated captures use Windows Chrome/WebGPU and report
  `requestAnimationFrame` presentation intervals. They do not expose GPU
  completion time, so no GPU-ms certification is claimed.
- The required 11.1 ms / 90 FPS target still needs a headed measurement on the
  specified Windows 11 / RTX 5070 Ti class target. Available-machine evidence
  is comparative, not certification.
- Physical controller buttons, Bluetooth reconnect behavior, touch hardware,
  speakers/headphones, and subjective mix balance require human/device review.

## Provenance and release scope

- The active RockerKaki runtime source is a clean owner-controlled Blender
  primitive replacement with a local palette and no remove.bg/network/imported
  geometry or texture step. The former Grok/remove.bg/Tencent chain remains
  documented as rejected, non-runtime audit history in `ASSETS.md`.
- The thirteen promoted Snow-Burgers ingredient, burger, rocket-chair, camp
  and dressing derivatives are checked by the structured expected-runtime
  manifest with exact runtime/source hashes. Their local procedural generation
  record and conditional output terms are retained; this is not a claim of
  copyrightability, exclusivity or blanket commercial clearance.
- The thirteen historical supplied Snow-Burgers/camp GLBs were rejected from
  active runtime because their source/licence metadata could not be
  established. They remain immutable audit history, separate from the promoted
  runtime derivatives; the human ledger and
  `public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` carry their source/license context.
- Generated promotional art is disclosed in structured records, including its
  source/derivative hashes, generation command record, applicable terms links
  and owner visual-review state. Visual review is not legal or commercial
  clearance.

## Gauntlet status

- `package.json`, the title footer, and the player-facing credits surface all
  report `v1.0.0`.
- Independent gameplay, camera, UI, progression/endgame, audio-core, asset,
  accessibility/control, release-gating, showreel, and final integration
  reviews cover the converged product. Those automated and critic passes do
  not replace the physical/human sign-offs listed above.
- The current branch is a reviewable release candidate. No merge, Pages publish
  or public release is implied by this document.
- A 72.240-second 1280×720 VP8 showreel is captured and independently passes
  the 60–90 second duration, required-beat, transition, and error gates. It has
  no audio stream and is visual presentation evidence only.
- In the darkest Midnight/Whiteout chase views, RockerKaki's small outfit and
  guitar details reduce to silhouette. The pale rear hair streak, horns, board,
  route, and HUD remain readable; the independent runtime critic classified
  this as a non-blocking P2 presentation limitation.
- Physical controller and touch-device ergonomics, human audio listening,
  human colour-vision review, and exact target-GPU certification remain release
  gates. These are external device/human checks, not unresolved runtime asset
  provenance.
