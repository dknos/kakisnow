RockerKaki — clean local procedural runtime source
==============================================================================

The default playable hero is generated locally by
`tools/generate-rockerkaki.py` with Blender 5.1.1 primitives and a repository-
local 64×64 palette. It has no imported geometry, imported texture, network
input, background-removal service, or third-party model dependency. The
generation record is `art/generated-assets/rockerkaki/GENERATION_RECORD.json`.

  Source GLB:  public/assets/models/rockerkaki.glb
      366,316 bytes
      SHA-256 c3a4c7325e86db85f43a8bc06694cc4621cec7a1acfb7ea8dbcb6dc01424b602
  Rigged GLB:  public/assets/models/rockerkaki-rigged.glb
      555,636 bytes
      SHA-256 c300cb1a1b581ac07d7f0319a4c0902340ee4d64520294a1ec4ac82fcb658007

The source records one material, 12,928 triangles and 8,962 exported rig
vertices.
The editable rig contains one root control, nine deform bones, and the
`RockerBreath` action. It was reviewed in Blender and in real Windows
Chrome/WebGPU gameplay; the full downhill smoke pass completed at 60 FPS with
zero console or GPU validation errors.

Historical audit note: the former Grok concept → remove.bg derivative →
Tencent HY 3D Global chain is retained as rejected, non-runtime history in
`ASSETS.md`. Its remove.bg account/plan could not be verified, so none of its
files is redistributed by this build. The clean local source above supersedes
that chain without erasing the audit record.
