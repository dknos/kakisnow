foundation's MIT notice is shipped at

`public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

## External and generated work

Runtime asset sources, creators, licenses, attribution, modifications and
hashes are recorded in `ASSET_LEDGER.md`, `ASSETS.md` and
`public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. No asset is considered cleared because it was
available for download or present on disk. The fixed expected-runtime inventory
and exact runtime/source hashes are machine-checked in
`art/generated-assets/snow-burgers/RUNTIME_MANIFEST.json`.

Some promotional 2D art is AI-assisted. The source prompt, model/workflow
record, date, edit relationship and runtime derivative are recorded alongside
the asset, with applicable terms links and owner visual-review state. This
disclosure does not assert copyrightability, exclusivity or blanket commercial
clearance, and it does not make an unresolved third-party model or image
provenance record resolved. The current strict release blocker is the
RockerKaki/remove.bg processing record described in `ASSETS.md`.

The thirteen promoted Snow-Burgers GLB derivatives were produced by the local
Blender primitive generator in an owner-directed AI-assisted Codex session;
their generation source, exact hashes, and conditional-output caveat are
recorded in the runtime manifest and asset ledger.

## Data and privacy

Snow-Burgers has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or
backend. Records, settings, bindings, ghosts, and Burger Book progress remain
in browser local storage. Save export/import is player-initiated and local; the
game does not upload player data.
